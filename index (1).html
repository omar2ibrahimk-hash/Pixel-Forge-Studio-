PixelForge Studio

  • Add: drag assets onto the page (desktop) or tap them (phone). Drop photos from your computer onto the page too.
  • Edit: drag to move, corners to resize, top dot to rotate. Arrow keys nudge; Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo/redo; Delete removes; Ctrl+D duplicates.
  • Video: each page is a clip on the timeline. Items can fade, slide or zoom in. WebM is recorded in your browser in real time, without audio.
  • Privacy: nothing is uploaded. Work autosaves in this browser only; clearing site data erases it, so use Save JSON for backups.
  • Not included (needs a backend or paid API): accounts, cloud sync, sharing links, stock photo/AI search, background removal, MP4 export, audio tracks. Fonts are system fonts and may look slightly different on other devices.
